other important inquiries:
- **how are these systems designed and what intended and unintended results are they creating?**
	- the matter of [[externalities\|externalities]], [[yellow teaming\|yellow teaming]] & [[evaluation\|evaluation]].
- **how might we design systems more consciously aligned with [[nature\|nature]], [[concepts/alchemy/self\|self]] and [[life\|life]] itself?**
	- the question of [[_002_core/design philosophies\|design philosophies/methodologies/approaches]].

these are huge questions, with valuable perspectives drawing from different disciplines, backgrounds and traditions, across time and space.

what i intend to present here aren't definitive answers, but curated perspectives i found useful to help me orient myself and better navigate complexity - that i believe can help us coordinate towards more omni-win futures and presents.
