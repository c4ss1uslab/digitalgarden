an extremely thorough conversation is due on this topic. for now, i'm just going to point out a few starting resources.

a few very good introductions to the theme (all from different angles and emphasizing different aspects of it):

- [[people/references/design/daniel schmachtenberger\|daniel schmachtenberger]]'s interview at [[concepts/design/the great simplification\|the great simplification]] - [Artificial Intelligence and The Superorganism](https://www.thegreatsimplification.com/episode/71-daniel-schmachtenberger)
- [[zak stein\|zak stein]]'s interview at the [[initiatives, orgs & communities/lab/center for humane technology\|center for humane technology]] - [Attachment Hacking and the Rise of AI Psychosis](https://www.humanetech.com/podcast/attachment-hacking-and-the-rise-of-ai-psychosis) and his [[AI psychological harms research coalition\|AI psychological harms research coalition]]
- [[responses/alchemy/meta-relationality\|metarelational.ai]]'s: [From Assumption to Inquiry: Mapping the Ontological Terrain of AI](https://decolonialfuturesnet.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/draft-mapping-the-ontological-terrain-of-ai.pdf) (both of the above are powerful and can be a bit dense, but this one might be even more challenging and mind-bending)

[[kate crawford\|kate crawford]]'s 2021 [[atlas of AI\|atlas of AI]] remains a key resource, along with other works with [[vladan joler\|vladan joler]], like 2018's [[anatomy of an AI system\|anatomy of an AI system]] and 2023's [[references/design/calculating empires\|calculating empires]].
[[karen hao\|karen hao]]'s investigative journalism on [[empire of AI\|empire of AI]] has also (of course) become subject for heated debate. much like [[shoshana zubboff\|shoshana zubboff]]'s work on [[surveillance capitalism\|surveillance capitalism]] and the attention economy. it's a good starting point.

more resources to come, but to jump into a very simple summary, let me share joep schuurukes' generative AI harms' checklist, to build upon it and explain a little bit of my somewhat contrarian position on it:

> ### [On the acceptance of GenAI](https://smallsheds.garden/blog/2026/on-the-acceptance-of-genai/)
> 
> — Joep Schuurkes, 5 April 2026
> 
> By using GenAI:
>
> - [ ]  I accept the models were trained on stolen data.
> - [ ] I accept that the data was labeled by exploited workers.
> - [ ] I accept the environmental costs of the data centers running these models.
> - [ ] I accept that I am outsourcing some of my skills to a company.
> - [ ] I accept these companies don’t have a viable business model.
> - [ ] I accept that I am granting more power to big tech and their vision for the world.
> - [ ] I accept that I am granting more power to the United States.
> - [ ] I accept that all this effort could have been spent elsewhere.
> 
> If any of these facts are new to you, you haven’t been doing your due diligence.
> 
> I have the deepest respect and sympathy for people who are forced by circumstances to accept these things.
> 
> I acknowledge that people’s ethical behavior is neither deterministic, nor consistent.
> 
> But yeah, GenAI radicalized me.

it's clearly an [[arms race\|arms race]]. everyone's trying to hoard chairs to sit when the music unavoidably stops.

i resonate with the [[responses/alchemy/meta-relationality\|metarelational.ai]] perspective. our individual or very intentional organizational use of it (with all its due precautions) is a very small subset of the harm that's already being caused.

if we ([[wide-boundary\|wide-boundary]]/[[concepts/alchemy/wisdom\|wisdom]]-oriented folks) can, through this tool*, gather power, resources and optionality that would otherwise be concentrated in the hands of a few corporations/individuals, it can be argued that we actually have the responsibility to do so.

\**AI is clearly not "just a tool". it's a tool and many more things at the same time. see: [Technology is Not Values Neutral: Ending the Reign of Nihilistic Design](https://consilienceproject.org/technology-is-not-values-neutral-ending-the-reign-of-nihilistic-design-2/)*.
