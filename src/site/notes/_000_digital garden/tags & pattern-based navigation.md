this page compiles a set of specific *tags* and opinionated [[patterns/pattern collections/knowledge design patterns\|knowledge design patterns]], that i'm using in this garden to help you better navigate all of the complex information presented here at different layers of depth.

##### tag-based navigation

most of the contents on this garden can be filtered/navigated through a few lenses:

**8 major domains/archetypes:** #alchemy, #design, #art, #lab, #player, #host, #regen, #music.
**4 stages of note maturity**: #🕳 *(very early, still not planted),* #🌱 *(early, seedling),* #🌿 *(growing, budding)* and #🌲 *(robust, evergreen).*
**5 major types of notes:**
- [[actors\|actors]]: #person, #organization, #institution, #initiative, #community, #network
- [[knowledge\|knowledge]]: #inquiry, #term, #concept, #topic, #field, #lineage, #perspective, #claim, #diagnosis, #heuristic, #framework, #methodology, #pattern , #reflection (personal), #datapoint
- [[tbprocessed/AI-generated/projects\|projects]]: #project, #tool, #experiment, #prototype, #response (an intentional response to the [[concepts/design/metacrisis\|metacrisis]] or similarly perceived global escalating challenges)
- [[sources\|sources]]: #article, #book, #course, #paper, #podcast, #video, #publication, #presentation, #website, #socialpost, #highlights, #resource (usually a website or a compilation of resources), #dataset
- original work/thought: #c4ss1us, #l1f3, #essay

soon:
1) **4 layers of application:** #personal, #local, #regional, #global.
2) **[[models & frameworks/design/three horizons\|3 horizons]]** of focus/application: #h1 *(short-term, reduce harm),* #h2 *(mid-term, empower/create supportive conditions),* #h3 *(long-term, design new systems, games and institutions).*

##### pattern-based navigation

notes also have a few other features ([[patterns/pattern collections/knowledge design patterns\|patterns]]) to help further filter and navigate them. these are still in the process of being added, sothey're still not present in many notes:

- [[patterns/active patterns/knowledge design/relevance score\|relevance scores]] (0-100) - my own judgement over how relevant this note is - both for myself and for anyone attempting to work towards the same vision of this garden (i.e. [[concepts/alchemy/thriving\|thriving]], [[regenerative\|regenerative]], more [[playful\|playful]], [[just\|just]], [[resilient\|resilient]], [[concepts/alchemy/wisdom\|wise]] and ultimately, [[sacred\|sacred]] realities).
	- relevance context - a description of: why is it relevant for me?
- [[aliveness score\|aliveness score]] (0-100) - different from relevance (which tends to be long-term), how much is this content alive in my current investigations?
- [[familiarity level \|familiarity level ]](0-100) - how familiar am i with the contents/topic/authors in that note?
	- familiarity context - a description of: what's my familiarity with it?
- [[cognitive complexity\|cognitive complexity]] (0-100) - how cognitively demanding this content might be.
	- complexity context - a description of: why can it be cognitively demanding?
- [[emotional density\|emotional density]] (0-100) - how emotionally demanding this content might be.
	- emotional context - a description of: why can it be emotionally demanding?
- [[information scope\|information scope]] (0-100) - how large is this body of work and/or other relevant, associated bodies of work necessary for making sense of this one?
- [[patterns/active patterns/epistemic status\|epistemic status]] (0-15) - how tested and validated (across multiple methods and perspectives) the contents and claims within that note are.
	- 1) unknown/uninvestigated,
	- 2) wild speculation,
	- 3) personally resonant,
	- 4) generally regarded as true within some communities,
	- 5) inter-subjectively validated,
	- 6) low levels of research conducted,
	- 7) basic level of research conducted,
	- 8) biases corrections sought out,
	- 9) foundational research conducted,
	- 10) peer-reviewed,
	- 11) large body of research/systematic research conducted,
	- 12) transdisciplinarily critiqued,
	- 13) transdisciplinarily evolved,
	- 14) anthro-ontologically verified,
	- 15) actively uninvisibilizing & anthro-ontologically verified
- [[confidence level\|confidence level]] (0-100) - contrasting with the epistemic status, this is a deeply personal judgment. i.e. contents and claims within a note can be considered established knowledge in a field. if i know nothing about it, my confidence level can be low.
		- confidence context - a description of: why is my confidence at that level? examples: supported by people i trust (in-group), validated by my experience* (self-validation), etc...
- [[hyperlinks\|hyperlinks]] - the clickable links throughout a note, which are related notes to dive deeper and feed your curiosity.
