this page compiles a set of specific *tags* and opinionated [[patterns/pattern collections/knowledge design patterns\|knowledge design patterns]], that i'm using in this garden to help you better navigate all of the complex information presented here at different layers of depth.

##### tag-based navigation

most of the contents on this garden can be filtered/navigated through a few lenses:

**8 major domains/archetypes:** #alchemy, #design, #art, #lab, #player, #host, #regen, #music.
**4 stages of note maturity**: #🕳 *(very early, still not planted),* #🌱 *(early, seedling),* #🌿 *(growing, budding)* and #🌲 *(robust, evergreen).*
**5 major types of notes:**
- [[actors\|actors]]: #person, #organization, #institution, #initiative, #community, #network
- [[knowledge\|knowledge]]: #inquiry, #term, #concept, #topic, #lineage, #perspective, #claim, #diagnosis, #heuristic, #framework, #methodology, #pattern , #reflection (personal)
- [[tbprocessed/AI-generated/projects\|projects]]: #project, #tool, #experiment, #prototype, #response (an intentional response to the [[concepts/design/metacrisis\|metacrisis]] or similarly perceived global escalating challenges)
- [[sources\|sources]]: #essay, #resource, #project, #topic, #response, #person, #organization, #institution.
- original: #c4ss1us, #l1f3

soon:
1) **4 levels [[cognitive complexity\|cognitive complexity]]**:  #absolutebeginner, #beginner, #intermediate, #advanced
2) **4 layers of application:** #personal, #local, #regional, #global.
3) **[[models & frameworks/design/three horizons\|3 horizons]]** of focus/application: #h1 *(short-term, reduce harm),* #h2 *(mid-term, empower/create supportive conditions),* #h3 *(long-term, design new systems, games and institutions).*

##### pattern-based navigation

notes also have a few other features ([[patterns/pattern collections/knowledge design patterns\|patterns]]) to help further navigate them:

- [[patterns/active patterns/knowledge design/relevance score\|relevance scores]] (0-100) - my own judgement over how relevant this note is in the context of the whole.
- [[patterns/active patterns/epistemic status\|epistemic status]] (0-10) - how confident i am over the contents and claims within the note.
- [[hyperlinks\|hyperlinks]] - related notes to dive deeper and feed your curiosity.

soon:
1) [[familiarity level \|familiarity level ]](0-100) - how familiar am i with the contents/topic/authors in that note?
2) [[aliveness score\|aliveness score]] (0-100) - different from relevance (which tends to be long-term), how much this specific content is alive in my current investigations?

and a few more to come...
